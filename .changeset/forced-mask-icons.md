The icons drawn as masks keep showing in forced colors, such as a Windows contrast theme. The browser paints every fill in the page's color there, the fill under a mask too, so the callout's icon, the arrow after a link that leaves the site, the prompt's chevron and the arrow of the docs theme's links out were gone. Each is now drawn in the system's color of the text it stands with: the callout's icon and the chevron in the text color, an arrow in its link's color. In the docs theme the same holds for the default theme's own icons of the links out, in the menu behind the three dots and in the phone's menu, for the three dots, and for the arrow after a link of the bar or the sidebar that leaves the site. Out of forced colors nothing changes. A page with these pieces or a site on the docs theme takes this with no change of its own. An icon you draw yourself from `css/icons.css` needs the same rule, which `docs/usage.md` and the file's comment give.
