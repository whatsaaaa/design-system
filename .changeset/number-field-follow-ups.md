The number field spec, still a draft, says what a click on a step button does and where its fill stops. A click on a step button puts the focus in the field, or keeps it there, so its arrow keys go on stepping the value and do not scroll the page: `tabindex="-1"` only takes a button out of the tab order, and a native button still takes the focus from a click unless the default of its `mousedown` is prevented. A touch on one leaves the focus where it is, so the system's keyboard does not open. A step button at its limit is disabled by `aria-disabled="true"`, not the native `disabled`, and a click on it does nothing: a native disabled button gets no `mousedown`, so a press on it took the focus out of the field, where a press on one that is `aria-disabled` keeps the focus in the field. In forced colors it is given `GrayText`, which the system gives only a native disabled button. The field's hover holds while the pointer is on a step button, since the buttons are inside its edge. At xs a step button's fill now reaches `space.4` past its icon on each side, in place of the control's gap, so the fill stays 2 px inside the 2 px error edge when a hover or a press raises it, as it does at every other size: the xs rows of the `decrement` and `increment` width are `calc(var(--ws-control-icon-size-xs) + var(--ws-space-4) + var(--ws-space-4))`. The icon and the value keep their place, and sm to xl are unchanged.

The parity helper now checks icons in forced colors: each part named `icon` or ending in `-icon` is drawn in a system color, such as `CanvasText` or `ButtonText`. An SVG keeps a color it is given there, so an icon in a color of the page's, such as a field's error icon in the error's color where the spec says it takes the system's text color, now fails. The check is of an icon drawn in SVG: the colors are the strokes and fills of its shapes. A gallery that passed before can fail this check after the update: that is a difference from the spec that the helper missed.

The style guide's checkout example draws Tickets as a number field, with its two named step buttons.
