Everything made for Whatsaaaa in the package is now under the MIT License, the identity artwork included: the ghost, the wordmark, the lockups and marks, the sprite and mark of each project, the README headers, the social cards, the profile banner and the favicons. A site that is not Whatsaaaa's own no longer has to hide the ghost. Third-party material keeps its own license, as listed in `licenses/README.md`, so the `license` field of `package.json` now reads `(MIT AND OFL-1.1)`. Versions already published keep the license they were published with.
