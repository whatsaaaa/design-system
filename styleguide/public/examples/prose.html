Conditions

The affects package adds conditions to definitions: a field, a section or a step shows, hides, or hides and clears its value, by the values of other fields.

Adding the extension

Conditions come from @whatsaaaa/form-builder-affects, an extension of the schema builder. Give the builder the extension:

Warning

Without the extension, affects is an unknown key: validate() reports it, and build() throws.

Effects

affects is a list of effects, each with the condition that applies it:

Effect While its condition is met
show the field shows. Otherwise it is hidden, like with hide
hide the field is hidden, and keeps its value
hideAndClear the field is hidden, and its value is cleared
  • Hide always wins. A field is hidden when its show condition isn't met.
  • Each effect goes once on a field. Join conditions with all or any instead.

Tip

Use hideAndClear for data that only makes sense while the field shows.
